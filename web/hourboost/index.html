WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT DO

Hour Boost keeps configured sessions active to accumulate playtime according to how Steam works.

It does not play matches for you, control gameplay, guarantee drops, trading cards, items, achievements or rewards, and it does not modify Steam servers.

HOUR BOOST FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
ABOUT HOUR BOOST.

Quick answers about how Hour Boost actually works inside Neith.

What is Hour Boost in Neith?+

Hour Boost keeps the sessions of the games you choose active so they accumulate playtime on Steam through Neith. You choose the account, the games and when the session starts or ends.

Does Hour Boost play matches for me?+

No. Hour Boost does not control gameplay or play matches. It also does not guarantee drops, trading cards, items, achievements or rewards.

What does Hour Boost FREE include?+

FREE allows 1 Steam account, 1 game and up to 2 hours per day. The quota becomes available again at the start of a new day.

What does Hour Boost PREMIUM include?+

PREMIUM allows up to 3 simultaneous accounts, up to 5 games per account and sessions with no daily limit.

Can I stop Hour Boost whenever I want?+

Yes. You can start or stop the session manually from Neith and check the timer, engine status and active games at any time.
