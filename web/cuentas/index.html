ACCOUNTS FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
ABOUT ACCOUNTS.

Everything you need to know about managing Steam accounts inside Neith Launcher PRO.

Can I manage multiple Steam accounts?+

Yes. Neith organizes multiple Steam profiles in one control center so you can access and switch between them in fewer steps.

Can I switch accounts quickly?+

Yes. The Accounts module is designed to make switching between saved Steam profiles faster and easier.

Does Neith mix data between my accounts?+

No. Each profile keeps its own identity and state within Neith.

How many accounts does Neith support?+

The FREE plan lets you use one account, while PREMIUM expands capacity to up to 20 stored accounts.

Are my credentials visible on the website?+

No. The website presents the launcher's features; actual account management is done inside Neith.
