Quick answers about Neith's free-game and promotion radar.

What does Free Games show?+

It shows active promotions and real opportunities to get games at no cost for a limited time.

Does Neith sell those games?+

No. Neith detects and presents the promotion, then directs you to the official store or source where it can be claimed.

Can I see how much time is left?+

Yes. When the information is available, Neith shows the remaining time or the promotion end date.

Is Free Games a FREE feature?+

Yes. Free Games browsing is available on the FREE plan.

Are the promotions permanent?+

Not necessarily. Many offers are temporary, which is why Neith highlights their status and remaining time whenever it can verify them.
