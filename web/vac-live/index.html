ABOUT VAC LIVE.

Quick answers about what VAC LIVE does, what information it shows and what it does not change.

What is VAC LIVE in Neith?+

It is the integrated Neith Launcher PRO view for checking and clearly presenting the available VAC status of your Steam accounts.

Does VAC LIVE remove or modify bans?+

No. VAC LIVE is an informational feature. It does not remove, modify or bypass Steam restrictions.

What information can it show?+

Depending on the available data, it can distinguish a clean account from an active ban and can show dates or countdowns when they exist.

Is VAC LIVE included in FREE?+

No. VAC LIVE is part of Neith Launcher PRO's PREMIUM features.

Can I check multiple accounts from Neith?+

Yes. VAC LIVE is integrated with Neith's account management so the entire check stays inside the same launcher.
