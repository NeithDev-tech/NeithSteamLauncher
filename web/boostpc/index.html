BOOST PC FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
ABOUT BOOST PC.

Quick answers about how Boost PC works and the types of optimizations Neith applies.

What does Boost PC do?+

It centralizes gaming-focused Windows profiles and optimizations, including process, memory, network and maintenance management.

Can the changes be reversed?+

The profiles are designed to apply controlled changes and let you return to a normal configuration when you finish gaming.

Does Boost PC replace Windows?+

No. It works on top of Windows and organizes settings and optimization actions from one unified interface.

Are there FREE and PREMIUM features?+

Yes. Neith provides a free core and reserves the most advanced optimizations and additional profiles for PREMIUM.

Do I need technical knowledge?+

No. The interface is designed so you can choose a profile and understand what Neith is doing without configuring every setting manually.
